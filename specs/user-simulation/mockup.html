      What this mockup shows

      • The layout of tools/sim-ui/ from design §5.9: arrivals and queue, a desk with 3 seats (PARALLEL=3), and the exit.
      • All data here is scripted sample data. The real page receives the same events from the bus over ws://localhost:8765/ws.
      • The Event stream tab lists every event in UI contract v1: persona_queue, walk_to_desk, user_say, bot_subagent, bot_tool, bot_token, bot_done, bot_interrupt, bot_error, persona_leave, session_graded, quota, batch_end.
      • The quota bars come from the rate_limit_event that claude -p reports. Once a bar passes the 85% mark, no new travellers are admitted.
