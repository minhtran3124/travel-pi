Lobby style study

The same scene drawn three ways: two travellers at Tin's desk, one bot answering, one tool running. Each direction follows a single rendering rule for every element in the scene.

Why the current mockup feels off: the travellers and the floor are low-resolution pixel art, but the speech bubbles, the text, and the counter labels are drawn smooth at full resolution. Mixing the two reads as neither retro nor modern. Each option below keeps one rendering rule for everything in the scene.

A · True pixel RPG retro, done fully

References: a16z AI Town, Stanford Smallville, Pixel Agents (Claude Code in VS Code), WorkAdventure.

  • The whole scene, bubbles included, is drawn into a 256×160 buffer and scaled up with nearest-neighbour.
  • Locked 16-colour palette, 16 px tiles, a 2-frame walk.
  • Bubbles only appear while someone is waiting on the other. Otherwise the status is shown by position: queue, desk, or exit.
GoodCozy and full of character, and it looks like the agent-town projects people already know.
CostLong replies do not fit in the scene; they belong in the side panel. Vietnamese diacritics are hard in a pixel font.

B · Flat isometric recommended

References: Kenney isometric sets, Linear and Stripe product illustrations, Gather's clean grid.

  • 2:1 isometric tiles drawn as vectors. Travellers are tokens (a capsule body and a round head) with no limbs.
  • The palette comes from DESIGN.md: ink, departure-lime, and the flight-status colours. Soft shadows, no outlines.
  • A ring under each character shows its status. Bubbles are rounded and set in Inter, matching the page.
GoodMatches the product's design system, reads cleanly at small sizes, and renders Vietnamese text in full.
CostLess playful than pixel art. Needs isometric depth sorting.

C · Soft chibi cute, pastel

References: Habbo's rooms, Townscaper's palette, cozy life-sim games.

  • Chibi travellers with big heads, soft gradient shading, and blush. Rounded furniture.
  • Desaturated pastels with one warm accent. Every shape has soft edges.
  • Activity is shown as floating emote icons (✈️ 🏨 💬) instead of text in the scene.
GoodFriendliest and most "demo-able" look.
CostDrifts furthest from the product's crisp design system, and has the most art to draw by hand.
